Residents urged to attend Greenvale planning meeting as decision nears

Campaigners are encouraging residents to attend Rochdale Borough Council's Planning Committee as councillors prepare to consider the Greenvale planning application for land south of Greenvale in Bamford.

By Rochdale Daily

Residents opposed to the proposed Greenvale development in Bamford are being encouraged to attend next week's Planning Committee meeting, where councillors are expected to consider the application for housing on land south of Greenvale. Campaigners say a strong public turnout will demonstrate the level of local concern surrounding the proposal.

The application, reference 25/00405/FUL, is listed as Item 7 on the agenda for Rochdale Borough Council's Planning Committee meeting on Wednesday, 5 August. The meeting is scheduled to begin at 6pm, with the Greenvale application expected to be considered during the evening.

The appeal has been organised by the Bamford Green Belt Action Group and supported by the Save Bamford Greenbelt campaign, which has urged residents to attend the meeting regardless of whether they intend to speak. In a public appeal, campaigners said simply being present would help demonstrate to committee members how important the decision is to the local community.

Campaigners argue there remain significant planning policy and evidence gaps which should be addressed before any decision is taken. They believe the proposed development would result in the unnecessary loss of Green Belt land and have consistently maintained that the site should remain protected from development.

The Green Belt surrounding Bamford has been the subject of longstanding debate as Rochdale Borough Council seeks to balance housing demand with the protection of open countryside. Supporters of Green Belt protection argue the land plays an important role in preventing urban sprawl, preserving the character of existing communities and providing valuable green space for wildlife and recreation. Those in favour of development argue additional housing is needed to meet growing demand and support future economic growth.

Planning Committee members are required to determine applications based on planning law and adopted planning policy rather than the popularity of a proposal. Material planning considerations can include matters such as highway safety, flood risk, ecology, landscape impact, heritage, design and compliance with local and national planning policies. Issues such as the identity of an applicant or the level of public support alone are not determinative, although representations submitted by residents are taken into account where they raise relevant planning matters.

Residents wishing to observe the meeting can attend in person, while those who have registered in advance may be permitted to address the committee in accordance with the council's procedures. Anyone intending to speak should check Rochdale Borough Council's guidance on registration deadlines and speaking arrangements before the meeting.

The committee's decision will determine whether planning permission is granted, refused or deferred pending further information. Whatever the outcome, the decision is expected to be closely watched by residents across Bamford and neighbouring communities, where the future of Green Belt land continues to generate strong public interest.
