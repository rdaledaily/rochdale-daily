Jolly Josh Christmas Ball sells out as Rochdale businesses urged to help

The Castleton charity is asking businesses to sponsor a table for £50 or donate raffle and auction prizes ahead of its December ball at Rochdale Town Hall.

By Rochdale Daily Newsdesk 20 Oct 2026 · 12:11 UTC

Rochdale charity Jolly Josh has filled the guest tables for its Jolly Jingle Christmas Ball and is now asking local businesses to help turn a full room into vital funding for families.

The ball takes place at Rochdale Town Hall on Friday 11 December at 7pm. With guest places filled, the charity is inviting businesses across Rochdale and Greater Manchester to sponsor tables for £50, donate raffle or auction prizes, or discuss other ways to support the evening.

A contribution does not have to be extravagant. Vouchers, meals, products, experiences and services can all help boost the fundraising total.

Based at Ensor Mill on Queensway in Castleton, Jolly Josh says it supports more than 300 families of children and young people with complex medical needs, physical disabilities, life limiting conditions, and profound and multiple learning disabilities.

Its purpose built centre offers hydrotherapy, rebound therapy, sensory activities, youth social groups, accessible after school provision and support for parents and carers.

The charity says it receives no regular government funding, making grants, donations, community fundraising and business support essential to keeping its services running.

Rose Hutchings, Lead Fundraiser at Jolly Josh, said:

“We’ve been absolutely delighted by the response to this year’s Jolly Jingle Ball and to see the tables fill. What we’d really love now is for the wider Rochdale business community to help us make the night as successful as possible for our families.

“A £50 table sponsorship or a donated raffle or auction prize might feel like a relatively small contribution to a business, but when lots of local organisations get behind us those contributions quickly add up.

“Jolly Josh doesn’t receive regular government funding, so fundraising and community support genuinely help us continue providing a safe, accessible place where children and young people with complex needs and their families can connect, access opportunities and feel part of a community.”

The appeal comes as demand for the charity’s specialist services continues to grow. Jolly Josh says its hydrotherapy provision has previously run at least 42 sessions a week and experienced waiting lists, with strong demand for other services too.

Businesses interested in a £50 table sponsorship, donating a raffle or auction prize, or offering other event support can email Rose Hutchings at rose@jollyjosh.co.uk or call 01706 399141.

Further information about Jolly Josh, registered charity number 1178116, is available at https://jollyjosh.co.uk/

Source: Jolly Josh press release supplied to Rochdale Daily.
