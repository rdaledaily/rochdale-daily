Horse in Rochdale left critically ill after being fed harmful food by campers

A horse owner in Rochdale is seeking awareness after her horse, Frank, fell critically ill due to being fed harmful scraps by campers. The incident has raised concerns about the dangers of feeding horses human food.

By Rochdale Daily Newsdesk

A horse in Rochdale has been left in a critical condition after being fed something dangerous by campers, according to his owner, Julie Kershaw. The incident occurred when Julie, 58, discovered her 13-year-old horse, Frank, trembling and unable to walk in the field where he is kept. Julie believes that the campers fed Frank human food, which led to his distress and subsequent health issues.

Julie was alerted to Frank's condition by her daughter, who was out walking with a friend when they found the horse shaking uncontrollably and struggling to breathe. They quickly issued a Facebook appeal to locate Julie, who rushed to the scene. Upon arrival, she found Frank in a dire state, fearing he would not survive the night. Julie stated, "He was really shaking and couldn't walk," highlighting the severity of the situation.

A vet was called and arrived within 40 minutes, administering four injections to treat Frank's condition, which was diagnosed as colitis, an infection in the colon. The emergency visit resulted in a vet bill of £235, with additional costs for food bringing the total to around £300. Julie expressed her frustration, stating, "It's just so scary - people don't realise how dangerous it is to feed horses."

This incident is not isolated; Julie noted that horse owners in the area have previously found harmful items such as chicken bones and vegetables discarded in fields. She urged the public to educate themselves on what is safe to feed horses, emphasising that even common treats can become dangerous if overfed. Julie's experience serves as a reminder of the potential risks associated with feeding horses human food.
