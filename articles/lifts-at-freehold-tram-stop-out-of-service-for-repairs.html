Failsworth tram stop lifts out of service during replacement works

The lifts at Freehold Tram Stop are currently out of service as replacement works take place, affecting passengers travelling towards Manchester and Rochdale.

By Rochdale Daily Newsdesk 18 Oct 2026 · 13:58 BSTUpdated 27 Oct 12:21 GMT
As it happenedLast updated 12:21 GMT, 27 October 20262 updates
12:21 GMT

The lifts at Failsworth tram stop are currently out of service due to ongoing replacement works. Passengers should plan alternative routes during this disruption.

11:12 BST

The lifts at Freehold Tram Stop are currently out of service as replacement works take place, affecting passengers travelling towards Manchester and Rochdale.

The lifts at Freehold Tram Stop are currently out of service, impacting passengers using the tram service towards Manchester and Rochdale. The disruption began on Saturday, 24 October 2026, at 10:19 am and will continue until further notice as lift replacement works are carried out.

Passengers are advised to alight at either South Chadderton or Westwood tram stops and use the orange call points located at all stops to request assistance for completing their journeys.

Read more: Failsworth Tram Stop Lift Out of Service Due to Replacement Works

For further assistance, customers can contact the helpline at 0161 244 1000, which operates from 7 am to 8 pm on weekdays and from 8 am to 8 pm on weekends and bank holidays.

This service disruption may affect those who rely on the tram for daily commutes or travel to local amenities. It is advisable for passengers to plan their journeys accordingly and consider alternative routes during this period.

Updates regarding the status of the lifts and any further developments will be provided through the Bee Network travel alerts. Passengers are encouraged to check the latest information as the situation evolves.

Background

Freehold tram stop, between South Chadderton and Oldham town centre, was built new on the trackbed of the old railway line.

Metrolink trams reached Rochdale railway station in February 2013 and Rochdale Town Centre, the end of the line, in March 2014.
