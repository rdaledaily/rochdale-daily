Stone Circle Near Rochdale Is a Long-Standing Local Landmark

Correction: An earlier version of this article incorrectly described the stone circle as newly installed. Local residents and walkers have confirmed that it has stood near Stoney Heys and Brownhouse Wham Reservoir for many years. Rochdale Daily apologises for the error.

The stone circle near Stoney Heys and Brownhouse Wham Reservoir is a familiar landmark to local walkers and residents.

The arrangement of stones has been present for many years and is well known to people who regularly use the surrounding paths. It should not have been presented as a recent installation.

Rochdale Daily has corrected the record and removed the inaccurate description.
