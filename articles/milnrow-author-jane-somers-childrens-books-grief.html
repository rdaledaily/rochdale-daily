Milnrow author helping children cope with grief through heartfelt storybooks

Milnrow author Jane Somers is helping children understand bereavement and pet loss through two beautifully illustrated storybooks, while donating hundreds of copies to hospitals and hospices.

By Rochdale Daily

A Milnrow author is helping children navigate some of life's most difficult moments through a pair of beautifully illustrated books exploring bereavement and the loss of much-loved pets.

Jane Somers has written two children's books, *Look For The Robin* and *Over Rainbow Bridge*, with the aim of providing comfort, reassurance and hope for young readers experiencing grief.

Drawing on gentle storytelling and colourful illustrations, Jane's books tackle subjects that many families find difficult to discuss, helping children understand loss in an age-appropriate and compassionate way.

Her first book, *Look For The Robin*, tells the story of a friendly robin who reminds grieving children that the special people they have lost are still watching over them and that they continue to be loved. The book has resonated with families, schools and support organisations, with Jane using proceeds from sales to give something back to the community.

Thanks to the support of readers, she has now donated more than 200 copies of the book to hospitals, hospices and other worthy causes, helping children facing bereavement access the story free of charge. Customers ordering directly from her website also receive a handmade robin clay heart trinket as a keepsake.

Jane's second book, *Over Rainbow Bridge*, focuses on another form of grief that is often underestimated – the loss of a family pet. Written in rhyme and accompanied by warm illustrations, the story follows the bond between children and their beloved animals, reassuring young readers that although their pet has crossed Rainbow Bridge, the love and memories they shared remain forever.

The book aims to help parents start conversations about pet loss in a gentle, positive way, recognising that the death of a much-loved animal is often a child's first experience of bereavement. Every copy purchased through Jane's online store includes a complimentary bookmark.

Child psychologists have long recognised the importance of helping children understand grief through honest but reassuring conversations. Books can provide a safe and accessible way for young readers to explore difficult emotions, ask questions and recognise that feelings such as sadness, confusion and loss are a normal part of bereavement.

Jane says her ambition has always been to create stories that offer comfort while giving families practical tools to support children during challenging times. By combining uplifting messages with beautiful illustrations, she hopes her books provide reassurance that love continues even after someone, or a cherished pet, has gone.

Both books retail at £9.99, excluding postage and packaging, and are available directly through Jane's website. As well as supporting an independent local author, every purchase helps her continue donating copies to hospitals, hospices and organisations supporting bereaved children.

Jane's work is another example of the creativity found within Rochdale borough, using the power of storytelling not only to entertain but to make a genuine difference in the lives of local families and children across the country.
