Rochdale Borough Council Set to Acquire Warwick Mill in Middleton

Rochdale Borough Council is preparing to take ownership of Warwick Mill, with a major regeneration announcement for Middleton town centre expected on 7 September.

By Rochdale Daily Newsdesk

Rochdale Borough Council is understood to be preparing to take ownership of Warwick Mill, a significant step in the ongoing regeneration efforts for Middleton town centre. While the acquisition has not yet been formally confirmed, details are anticipated to be revealed at the 'Our Town, Our Future' event scheduled for Monday, 7 September.

The event will take place at Middleton Arena and is expected to include a major announcement regarding the regeneration of the town centre, alongside the results of a consultation involving nearly 2,500 residents and businesses. This consultation aims to gather local input on future developments and improvements.

In addition to the Warwick Mill announcement, the event will also highlight the first year of the Middleton Co-operative Mayoral Development Corporation. Key figures, including Rose Marley and Steve Coogan, co-chairs of the development corporation, will be in attendance, along with Rochdale Borough Council leader Councillor Neil Emmott and councillors June West and Dylan Williams.

The council was approached for comment regarding the acquisition but did not respond by the time of publication. Local residents and businesses are encouraged to attend the event to learn more about the future of Middleton town centre and the role Warwick Mill will play in its revitalisation.
