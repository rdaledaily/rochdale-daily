Free job-search, CV and money advice at Middleton Library today

Middleton Library is hosting a free work and skills hub on Monday 9 November, with help for job applications, interviews and managing budgets.

By Rochdale Daily 9 Nov 2026 · 09:05 GMT

People looking for work or help improving their job applications can attend a free work and skills session at Middleton Library on Monday 9 November 2026.

Rochdale Borough Council lists the session from 9.30am until 12.30pm at Middleton Library, Long Street, Middleton, M24 6DU. No booking is needed and adults can simply turn up.

The council says the hub offers advice on finding vacancies and training courses, completing CVs and application forms, preparing for interviews, and managing debt or household budgets.

The session is described as taking place every other Monday, although residents should check the council's events listing for the next confirmed date rather than assuming every fortnightly date will go ahead.

More from Middleton: Bikers in red leave Birch Services for 13th Ring of Red ride around the M60

For someone returning to employment, the most useful starting point may be a CV or a specific application they want help with. Anyone seeking support can bring details of the vacancy or course they are interested in, although the council does not state that any paperwork is required.

The council's listing confirms the session is free. For enquiries, contact middleton.library@rochdale.gov.uk.

Source: Rochdale Borough Council event listing, https://www.rochdale.gov.uk/events/event/579/work-and-skills-hub---middleton-library
