Milnrow's John Milne Table Table to close on September 3

John Milne Table Table in Milnrow will close on 3 September, with the restaurant thanking generations of customers for their support and inviting guests to visit once more.

By Rochdale Daily Newsdesk 31 Aug 2026 · 23:19 UTC

This is a developing story. We'll publish more details as they emerge.

John Milne Table Table in Milnrow will officially close its doors on Thursday, 3 September, as Table Table prepares to close its locations.

In a farewell message to guests, the restaurant said it had been an honour to serve communities across the UK, from quick mid-week dinners to decades of family celebrations.

The team thanked customers who had made the restaurant feel like home and said it hoped local diners would be able to visit at least once more and create more memories before the doors close.

The message ended simply: "Thank you for everything."
