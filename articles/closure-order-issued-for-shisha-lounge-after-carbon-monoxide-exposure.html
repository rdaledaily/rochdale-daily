Shisha Lounge Closed After Carbon Monoxide Exposure

Phantom The Lounge has been shut down after a court order found excessive carbon monoxide levels posed a risk to customers and staff.

By Rochdale Daily Newsdesk

A shisha lounge in Rochdale has been closed following a court order due to dangerous levels of carbon monoxide found on the premises. Phantom The Lounge, located on Maclure Road, was ordered to cease operations by Rochdale Council's environmental health service after a routine inspection revealed serious violations of health regulations.

The closure order was granted at Manchester Magistrates' Court on July 24, based on evidence submitted by environmental health officers, with support from Greater Manchester Fire and Rescue Service and Greater Manchester Police. The inspection found that the lounge had been illegally operating by allowing smoking in a substantially closed area, which contravenes the Smoke-Free regulations under the Health Act 2006.

In addition to the illegal smoking, the inspection uncovered hazardous practices in the kitchen that exposed customers and staff to excessively high levels of carbon monoxide. An electric fan was reportedly used to smoulder coals in an unventilated area, resulting in carbon monoxide levels exceeding 100 parts per million (PPM) in the kitchen. On two occasions, fire officers had to instruct customers and staff to leave the premises for their safety.

Rochdale Council stated that they had offered the owners an opportunity to rectify the issues, but the business refused to cooperate. This lack of compliance led to the council's environmental health service taking court action as a last resort. Councillor Tricia Ayrton, deputy leader and portfolio holder for Climate Change and Environment, emphasised the importance of health regulations in ensuring the safety of both customers and workers.
