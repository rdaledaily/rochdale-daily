Birthday cards for Wendy: family’s heartfelt appeal ahead of her 79th birthday

Wendy Bamford turns 79 on Wednesday 4 November, and her birthday wish is simple: cards filled with kind words.

By Rochdale Daily Newsdesk · 31 October 2026 · 15:02 GMT

A family is inviting the community to help make Wendy Bamford’s 79th birthday special by sending her a card filled with a few kind words.

Wendy turns 79 on Wednesday 4 November. Her son Stephen Bamford shared the appeal after she moved to Springhill Hospice in Rochdale.

When he asked his mum what she wanted for her birthday, or whether there was anything she would like to do, her answer was simple: lots of birthday cards, so she could sit and read the lovely messages inside. It is the only thing she has asked for, Stephen said.

A little kindness could mean so much

Stephen is welcoming cards from people who know Wendy and those who have never met her but would like to brighten her day.

“A few kind words might seem like a small thing, but they would mean the world to her right now,” he wrote.

In an update, Stephen thanked everyone for their comments and messages, explaining that the response had become too large for him to answer individually. He shared two addresses where cards can be sent.

Where to send a birthday card

Cards can be addressed directly to Wendy at:

Wendy Bamford
Room 10
Springhill Hospice
Broad Lane
Rochdale

Alternatively, cards can be sent to Stephen through the Littleborough collection address:

For Wendy Bamford, care of Stephen Bamford
Cryers Butchers
39 Victoria Street
Littleborough
OL15 8BA

Stephen has promised to make sure his mum receives them, ready to open and enjoy on her birthday. Anyone wishing to take part is encouraged to allow time for delivery.
