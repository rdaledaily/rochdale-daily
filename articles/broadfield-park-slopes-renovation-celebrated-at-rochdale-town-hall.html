Broadfield Park Slopes renovation celebrated at Rochdale Town Hall

A special event at Rochdale Town Hall recognised the contributions of volunteers and partners involved in the restoration of Broadfield Park Slopes, highlighting the project's community impact and heritage significance.

By Rochdale Daily Newsdesk 17 Oct 2026 · 08:23 BST

A celebration was held at Rochdale Town Hall to honour the individuals and groups who contributed to the renovation of Broadfield Park Slopes.

This historic site, located between Rochdale Town Hall and St Chad’s Church, has undergone significant restoration, thanks to the efforts of local volunteers and funding from The National Lottery Heritage Fund.

The event, organised by Polly Brant, Cultural Engagement Officer for Rochdale Council, brought together volunteers, partners, and community members to acknowledge their roles in the project. Guests were treated to a series of films by local filmmaker Jack Grindrod, showcasing the stories and heritage associated with the park's transformation. The films captured the community involvement that played a crucial role in the renovation process.

The evening featured poetry readings by Alende Amisi, a local poet and former young poet in residence, who shared his personal reflections on Rochdale's heritage.

His contributions added a personal touch to the event, which celebrated not only the park's restoration but also the community spirit that drove the project forward.

The renovation has restored the historic Packer Spout Fountain, created a new accessible pathway, improved landscaping, and introduced new heritage interpretation.

The lower lawn has been terraced to create a natural amphitheatre, providing a new space for activities and events, enhancing the park's role as a community hub.

Background

Rochdale Town Hall, designed by William Henry Crossland, opened on 27 September 1871 and is Grade I listed.

Rochdale Town Hall closed in 2020 for a conservation project that ran until 2024, and the restoration won a Europa Nostra Award in 2026.
