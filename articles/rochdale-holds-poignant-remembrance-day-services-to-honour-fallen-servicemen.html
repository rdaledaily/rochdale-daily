Rochdale holds poignant Remembrance Day services to honour fallen servicemen

Thousands gather across the borough to pay their respects during Remembrance Day services, acknowledging the sacrifices made by servicemen and women.

By Rochdale Daily Newsdesk 8 Nov 2026 · 12:01 GMT

Thousands of residents gathered across Rochdale on 8 November 2026 to participate in Remembrance Day services, honouring the sacrifices made by servicemen and women.

The services took place at various memorials throughout the borough, with poignant moments observed as the community came together to remember the fallen.

Elsie Blundell, MP for Heywood and Middleton North, highlighted the importance of these observances, stating that it is crucial to remember those who served.

Rochdale Council acknowledged the significance of the Remembrance Day moment, reinforcing the community's commitment to honouring its heroes. The phrase 'Lest we forget' resonated throughout the ceremonies, reminding attendees of the sacrifices made for their freedom.

These remembrance activities are part of a national observance, reflecting the collective gratitude and respect for those who have served. The turnout at the services demonstrates the community's dedication to remembering its history and the individuals who have shaped it.
