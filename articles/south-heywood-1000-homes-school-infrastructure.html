South Heywood’s 1,000-home masterplan: when will the promised community facilities arrive?

South Heywood combines an ambition for 1,000 homes with employment development and new community facilities. Russell LDP’s masterplan describes a primary school, local centre, sports pitches, play areas and a 2.2-kilometre link road. Its original hybrid permission dates from March 2020.

By Rochdale Daily Newsdesk 14 Oct 2026 · 11:51 UTC

South Heywood combines an ambition for 1,000 homes with employment development and new community facilities. Russell LDP’s masterplan describes a primary school, local centre, sports pitches, play areas and a 2.2-kilometre link road. Its original hybrid permission dates from March 2020.

The June 2026 council update records continuing Bellway and Anwyl construction and approval of Northstone’s next phase. The link road has opened, according to the project’s announcement. Older forecasts on the masterplan webpage should not be presented as current construction dates.

For residents, the issue is the sequence of delivery. Roads serving new housing and logistics operations do not by themselves establish that classrooms, bus services or medical appointments will be available when families move in. Those questions require the legal obligations, funding and occupation thresholds for each phase.

The published material reviewed does not establish the total number of secured affordable homes or their rents. Individual phases must therefore be assessed separately. Northstone’s homes are already within the 1,000-home masterplan and cannot be added again.

Bellway joined the CMA’s 2025 commitments settlement, closed without an infringement finding. This does not establish a breach at the local development.

Location: South Heywood

Planning stage: Masterplan consent secured; housing delivered in phases

Homes: Up to 1,000 across the masterplan, including separate builder phases

Let or for sale: Market homes and any agreed affordable provision; masterplan-wide secured breakdown not established

Land: Originally a primarily Green Belt site; not a wholly brownfield regeneration scheme

Final planning agreement: The executed Section 106 deed, complete schedules, payment triggers and later variations were not verified. This is an evidence gap, not a claim that no agreement exists.

Exact rents / prices: No exact future rent, sale price, service charge or estate-charge schedule was established from the council/government material reviewed; no reliable scheme-specific current quotation was identified.

Affordable breakdown: No masterplan-wide signed tenure total verified. Bellway, Anwyl and Northstone phases must be reconciled within, rather than added to, the 1,000-home ceiling.

Funded school capacity: A primary school is in the masterplan. The December 2025 Heywood forecast is reproduced in the overview; it is not a funded school-opening commitment. A scheme-specific construction budget and opening trigger were not verified.

Funded healthcare capacity: No site-specific funded NHS expansion, additional staffed appointment capacity or delivery date was established from the accessible council/government records reviewed. Proximity to a surgery is not a capacity assessment.

Funded transport capacity: The 2.2-kilometre Heywood link road has opened: a delivered infrastructure asset. This does not establish additional school or NHS capacity, or a funded increase in bus frequency.

The outstanding details identified above are not yet available to Rochdale Daily. This article will be updated when the information becomes available.

Research checked 14 October 2026. Advertised prices are snapshots, not achieved sale prices.
