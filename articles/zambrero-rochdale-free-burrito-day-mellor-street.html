Zambrero to give away free burritos at new Mellor Street restaurant in Rochdale

Zambrero will hand out hundreds of free burritos and bowls from 11am to 2pm on Thursday 12 November to mark its new Mellor Street restaurant, which opened in September.

By Rochdale Daily Newsdesk 3 Nov 2026 · 13:55 GMT

Zambrero is giving away free burritos and bowls at its new Mellor Street restaurant in Rochdale next week, as a thank-you to the town six weeks after it opened.

The Free Burrito Day takes place on Thursday 12 November, from 11am to 2pm. The company says it will hand out hundreds of freshly made burritos and bowls during the three-hour window, and is advising people to arrive early to avoid the queues. Zambrero's resident scratch DJ, NC, will be playing hip hop and crowd favourites throughout.

Read more: Zambrero Opens New Mexican Restaurant on Mellor Street, Rochdale

The restaurant opened on 25 September and is Zambrero's first site in Rochdale. It offers dine-in, takeaway and delivery, has seating for 18, and is close to the town centre and local colleges. According to the company, the opening has created six jobs.

The menu centres on burritos and bowls, alongside tacos, nachos and quesadillas, made to order at the counter. Customers can choose from slow-cooked fillings, salad, rice, beans and toppings, and there are vegetarian, vegan and halal options.

The site also brings the chain's Plate 4 Plate scheme to Rochdale, under which a meal is donated to someone in need for every burrito or bowl bought. Zambrero says the scheme has provided more than 110 million meals worldwide.

Damian Bennett, Zambrero's development agent for Greater Manchester and West Yorkshire, said: "We've had a fantastic response since opening our doors in Rochdale and this Free Burrito Day is our way of thanking the local community for its support. We can't wait to welcome residents, students and local workers to come along, enjoy a free burrito or bowl and experience what Zambrero is all about."

He added that every burrito and bowl given away on the day, and bought afterwards, would count towards the Plate 4 Plate programme.

Rochdale is Zambrero's 18th UK opening since the brand arrived in Britain in 2021, with other sites in London, Manchester, Birmingham, Reading, Essex, Basingstoke, Northampton and Glasgow. The company was founded in Australia in 2005 and now has more than 350 locations worldwide.
