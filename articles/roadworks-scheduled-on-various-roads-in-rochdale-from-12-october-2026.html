Castleton night works: Manchester Road’s three traffic phases

Overnight restrictions run until 8 November. The council has not published dates for individual phases.

By Rochdale Daily Newsdesk 28 Sep 2026 · 16:04 UTC

This is a developing story. We'll publish more details as they emerge.

Manchester Road drivers face overnight restrictions for Bee Network Phase 2 resurfacing, scheduled from 12 October to 8 November 2026, 8pm–5am. Only one phase operates at a time.

Phase 1 closes Manchester Road between Onslow Street and Bolton Road. Silk Street is westbound-only from Tesco to Manchester Road; right turns from Bolton Road onto Manchester Road are banned.

Phase 2 bans north-eastbound travel on Manchester Road from Bolton Road to Roch Valley Way. There is no left turn from Bolton Road onto Manchester Road. The old Manchester Road section must exit via Roch Valley Way. Manchester Road traffic can only turn left onto Bolton Road. Westbound travel on Silk Street from Tesco to Manchester Road is prohibited.

Phase 3 bans south-westbound travel between Roch Valley Way and Bolton Road. There is no left turn from Edinburgh Way onto Manchester Road, or right turn from Roch Valley Way. Silk Street closes between Manchester Road and Tesco, with no right turn into it from Manchester Road.

Diversions will be signed. Check the current phase before travelling: https://www.rochdale.gov.uk/directory-record/2618/various-roads-roadworks-in-rochdale-from-12-october-2026
