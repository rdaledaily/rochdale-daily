Mother Hubbard's Fish and Chips Opens in Middleton with 45p Offer

Hundreds queued for hours outside Middleton Shopping Centre on Saturday to enjoy 45p fish and chips from the newly opened Mother Hubbard's, marking a nostalgic price from 1972.

By Rochdale Daily Newsdesk

ONGOING STORY — Updated 12 September 2026 at 18:29. 4 source updates have been combined into this article.

A new branch of Mother Hubbard opened in Middleton Shopping Centre on Saturday, 12 September 2026, drawing significant crowds as the fish and chip franchise offered a special promotion of 45p fish and chips for the first 1,000 customers. The event saw customers queuing back to the bus station, eager to take advantage of the deal.

Among the first to enjoy the new menu was Colin Thompson, who praised the quality of the food and the hard work of the staff on social media. Other customers echoed his sentiments, expressing excitement about the new addition to the local dining scene.

In a show of community support, the nearby Middleton Fish Bar also experienced a surge in business, selling out of its offerings and closing early at 2.30pm. Owners Paul and Caroline High thanked their customers for the overwhelming response, highlighting the importance of community engagement in their success.

The opening of Mother Hubbard and the success of Middleton Fish Bar signal a growing interest in fish and chip shops in the area, providing local residents with more dining options. As both establishments continue to attract customers, it remains to be seen how this will impact the local food scene in Middleton.
