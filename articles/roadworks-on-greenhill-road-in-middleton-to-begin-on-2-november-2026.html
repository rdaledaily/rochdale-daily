Joy Street Roadworks Scheduled from 2 November 2026

Joy Street in Rochdale will be temporarily closed for roadworks from 2 November to 16 November 2026, affecting local traffic flow and access to surrounding roads.

By Rochdale Daily Newsdesk 20 Oct 2026 · 00:52 UTC

This is a developing story. We'll publish more details as they emerge.

Joy Street in Rochdale will be temporarily closed for roadworks from 8am on Monday, 2 November 2026, until 5pm on Monday, 16 November 2026. The closure will extend from the junction with Binns Nook to the junction with Scarr Drive, impacting local traffic flow.

The roadworks are part of ongoing improvements by Rochdale Borough Council aimed at enhancing the infrastructure in the area. Residents and commuters are advised to plan their journeys accordingly, as access to Scarr Drive, Dewhirst Road, and Binns Nook Road may also be affected during this period.

This closure is expected to cause some disruption, particularly for those who rely on Joy Street for daily travel. Local businesses and residents are encouraged to stay updated on the progress of the works through the council's communications.

For more information about the roadworks and to view a map of the affected area, visit the Rochdale Borough Council website. Being aware of these changes will help residents avoid delays and ensure safe travel during the construction period.
