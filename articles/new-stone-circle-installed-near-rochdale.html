Stone Circle Near Rochdale Is a Long-Standing Local Landmark

The stone circle near Stoney Heys and Brownhouse Wham Reservoir has been familiar to local residents and walkers for many years.
By Rochdale Daily · Updated 4 August 2026
Correction: An earlier version of this article incorrectly described the stone circle as newly installed. Local residents have confirmed that it has stood at the site for many years. Rochdale Daily apologises for the error.

A stone circle on the hillside near Stoney Heys and Brownhouse Wham Reservoir is a well-known feature of the local landscape rather than a recent installation.

Walkers and residents familiar with the area say the arrangement has been present for many years and is regularly encountered along routes across the surrounding moorland.

The site features a group of standing stones arranged around a central stone structure. Its position and appearance have made it a recognisable landmark for people using the nearby paths.

Rochdale Daily has amended the article to reflect the information supplied by local people with direct knowledge of the site.
