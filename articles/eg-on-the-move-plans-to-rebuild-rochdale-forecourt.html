EG On The Move plans to rebuild Rochdale forecourt

EG On The Move has announced plans for a significant redevelopment of a filling station in Rochdale, aiming to enhance its services and facilities.

By Rochdale Daily Newsdesk 4 Nov 2026 · 15:03 GMT

EG On The Move has unveiled plans to demolish and rebuild a filling station located on the A58 in Rochdale, Greater Manchester. This initiative is part of the company's ongoing growth strategy, which aims to modernise its facilities and improve customer service.

The proposed redevelopment will involve a complete knock-down of the existing structure, followed by the construction of a new forecourt. This project is expected to enhance the overall customer experience at the site.

The plans are currently under review, and further details regarding the timeline for the project have yet to be announced. Local residents and businesses may be affected during the construction phase, but the company aims to minimise disruption.

As the project progresses, updates will be provided to keep the community informed.
