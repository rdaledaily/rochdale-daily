Rochdale AFC suffers 4-2 defeat at Port Vale

Rochdale AFC suffered a disappointing 4-2 defeat against Port Vale, with early goals sealing the match early on.

By Rochdale Daily Newsdesk 27 Oct 2026 · 12:32 GMT

Port Vale secured a convincing 4-2 victory over Rochdale AFC in a League Two match on Saturday, October 31, 2026. The game, held at Vale Park, saw the home side take control early, scoring three goals within the first 22 minutes.

Onel Hernandez opened the scoring just 90 seconds into the match, finishing a through-ball from Kyle Dempsey. Port Vale doubled their lead shortly after, with George Byers converting a penalty awarded for a foul on Hernandez.

The third goal came when Jordan Gabriel capitalised on a rebound after a shot from Dempsey was parried by Rochdale's goalkeeper Sam Waller.

Despite a brief threat from Rochdale, when Cian Hayes set up Dajaune Brown, the visitors struggled to find their rhythm. The second half saw fewer chances, but Ian Henderson managed to score for Rochdale in the 82nd minute.

Read more: Rochdale Hornets Suffer Heavy Defeat to Midlands Hurricanes

However, Keyrol Figueroa restored Vale's three-goal cushion just minutes later, before Charlie Tasker added a second for Rochdale with the last kick of the game.

This defeat leaves Rochdale needing to regroup as they prepare for their next fixture. Manager Ian Watson noted the team's defensive lapses, stating that they conceded 'soft goals' which ultimately cost them the match.

Fans will be looking for improvements in their upcoming games to climb the League Two table.

Background

Dale returned to the Football League in May 2026, beating Boreham Wood on penalties at Wembley in the National League play-off final after finishing second with a club-record 106 points.

The family of local businessman Peter Ogden completed a takeover of the club in May 2024; Simon Gauge and Cameron Ogden are co-chairmen.
