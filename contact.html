The newsroom

Contact Rochdale Daily

Send a story, challenge something we have published, list an event or talk to us about your organisation.

Local newsdesk We are a small newsroom covering the Rochdale borough. Every address on this page reaches a person, and we read all of it.

Newsroom directory

Choose the desk that best fits your message.

01 · Newsdesk

Send us a story

If something is happening in your street, school, club or business, tell us. You do not need to write it up — a few lines and a location are enough, and a photograph helps.

news@rochdaledaily.co.uk

If you would rather not be named, say so. If what you are sending is sensitive, tell us and we will agree how to handle it before publication.

02 · Standards

Corrections & complaints

If we have got something wrong, tell us and we will put it right. We publish corrections openly rather than quietly editing a page.

cc@rochdaledaily.co.uk

This address also covers right of reply and complaints about reporting or reader comments. Read our corrections and complaints process.

03 · What’s on

List an event

Community events are free to list. The quickest route is the Add an event form on the homepage, which goes straight into our listings queue.

events@rochdaledaily.co.uk
04 · Community

Groups & charities

If you run a group, food bank, support service or volunteer project in the borough, tell us so we can cover your work and keep our community information useful.

community@rochdaledaily.co.uk
05 · Commercial

Advertise

Advertising keeps Rochdale Daily free to read. We carry a small number of placements and list local trades and services in our directory.

advertising@rochdaledaily.co.uk

Advertisers have no influence over what we report. Buying space does not buy coverage or protection from coverage; see our terms.

06 · Your information

Privacy requests

To ask what personal information we hold about you, request a correction or deletion, or raise anything covered by our privacy policy, contact the newsdesk.

news@rochdaledaily.co.uk
07 · Accessibility

Help using the site

If any part of the site is hard to use, or you need something in a different format, tell us what you were trying to do and what happened. See our accessibility statement.

news@rochdaledaily.co.uk

Not sure where it belongs?

Send it to the newsdesk. We will pass it to the right place.

news@rochdaledaily.co.uk

Response time

We aim to reply within two working days. Rochdale Daily is edited anonymously; that does not affect our responsibilities as a publisher, and every message reaches the editor.

Last updated: 30 August 2026. We review this page at least once a year, and whenever we change how the site works.
