Contact us

We are a small newsroom covering the Rochdale borough. Everything below reaches a person, and we read all of it.

Send us a story

If something is happening in your street, your school, your club or your business, tell us. You do not need to write it up — a few lines and a location is enough, and a photograph helps.

news@rochdaledaily.co.uk

If you would rather not be named, say so and we will not name you. If you are sending something sensitive, tell us that too and we will agree how to handle it before we do anything with it.

Corrections and complaints

If we have got something wrong, tell us and we will put it right. We publish corrections openly rather than quietly editing a page.

cc@rochdaledaily.co.uk

The same address covers a right of reply, and any complaint about our reporting or about a reader comment. Our corrections and complaints page explains what happens next.

List an event

Community events are free to list. The quickest route is the Add an event form on our homepage, which goes straight into our listings queue.

events@rochdaledaily.co.uk

Community groups and charities

If you run a group, a food bank, a support service or a volunteer project in the borough, we want to know about it — both to cover your work and to make sure our community support pages point people to the right place.

community@rochdaledaily.co.uk

Advertise

Advertising is what keeps this site free to read. We carry a small number of placements so that each one is actually seen, and we list local trades and services in our directory.

advertising@rochdaledaily.co.uk

Advertisers have no influence over what we report. Buying space does not buy coverage, and it does not protect anyone from being written about — that is set out in our terms.

Your information

To ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to raise anything else covered by our privacy policy:

news@rochdaledaily.co.uk

Accessibility

If any part of this site is hard to use, or you need something in a different format, tell us what you were trying to do and what happened. See our accessibility statement.

news@rochdaledaily.co.uk

Not sure which one?

Use the newsdesk address and we will pass it on.

news@rochdaledaily.co.uk

We aim to reply within two working days. Rochdale Daily is edited anonymously; that does not affect our responsibilities as a publisher, and every message reaches the editor.
