# Rochdale Daily readership desk

## Daily search-led reporting

The Codex daily newsroom run uses the connected Search Console account at 07:30 local time. Record its actual data window and settled date. Inspect query and landing-page rows together; do not add query rows to estimate total traffic. Start with high-impression practical questions and the existing canonical article. Verify the source again before changing prices, opening times, dates or eligibility. Update useful coverage in place; do not create one duplicate page per keyword variation. Publish at most one genuinely new sourced report per run. If evidence is absent, record the gap instead of filling it with guesses.

The first identified opportunities are SpudBros practical information, local business changes, event arrangements and travel. These are research assignments, not verified facts. Refresh date-sensitive material before social distribution. Search demand does not justify allegations, invented reaction, medical advice or manufactured reporting.

## Reporting that earns repeat visits

Use reader_usefulness.py in the existing house-style prompts: answer the main finding early, then give the local consequence, evidence and confirmed next step. For council money, planning, services and promises, retain a follow-up question and the document/decision needed to answer it. Real original reporting requires evidence gathering. Attribute documents and responses, distinguish proposals from decisions, and do not describe a rewrite as an investigation or interview.

Run `python scraper/readership_desk.py` to prepare up to twelve recent-article social drafts and follow-up assignments in reports/readership-desk.json. It never posts, emails or invents facts. Editors check that the social post, headline, standfirst and opening paragraph deliver the same promise. A useful direct article link carries source/medium campaign tags; never collect a reader's identity in those tags.

## Reader journeys

The start-here.html page offers area/subject discovery, optional on-device preferences, a dated published briefing example, newsletter signup, RSS, existing browser alerts, newsroom questions and trust/correction links. Local preferences do not create a subscription or automatic issue alert. Use established ward/locality pages rather than duplicate thin location pages. The article read-next lead ranks subject relevance ahead of generic borough recency. Dates remain visible. Do not label historic reporting as current arrangements.

The site already has ward pages, official events, source links, correction routes and archive preservation. Keep those systems; verify useful coverage across neighbourhoods rather than fill every page to an arbitrary story quota. No photographs are changed by this work.

## Delivery prerequisites

The public reader-integrations endpoint currently reports newsletter and browser-push providers unconfigured. Newsletter signup can save consent; this is not proof an email will arrive. To activate existing delivery, the owner must connect a Brevo account/list, verify the sender and supply the API key as a secret in Cloudflare and the existing GitHub newsletter workflow. Configure the existing OneSignal application separately for notifications. Do not put keys in source files, create credentials by guesswork, activate billing, import contacts without consent or claim a topic subscription exists when it does not. No campaign or outreach is sent by this implementation.

## Measurement and experiments

GA4 is connected. Baseline data was recorded only from 9–18 October: 734 measured users, 822 sessions, 1,048 views and 38.4% engagement overall. Consent and blocked tracking omit readers; these are not a count of all unique people. Compare like-for-like settled periods and mobile separately. Track returning users, engaged sessions by source, newsletter saves and related-story clicks. New reader events are consent-gated and send only a fixed UI slot, never form values, postcode, email or article text. newsletter_signup_saved means the backend saved a signup; it is not a confirmed delivered subscription or revenue event.

For four weeks, change one social presentation or mobile journey at a time, record the date and compare source/device cohorts. Do not infer improvement from a small ten-day sample or compare Cloudflare visits directly with GA4 users. Keep GSC query evidence and audience figures in private reporting outputs, not a public marketing claim. No numeric growth target is promised before a stable baseline.

## Community distribution

Prepare a short, relevant introduction for a school, club, charity or community group explaining the exact useful coverage and inviting corrections or tips. Offer a public link and an optional route to keep in touch. Do not mass-message groups, scrape member contacts, post without permission or treat this plan as authorization to send outreach. Readers can submit questions free through the start page; paid reader submissions remain clearly separate from independent reporting.
