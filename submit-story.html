Submit your own story

This is a paid reader-submission service, not independent newsroom coverage. Accepted stories are labelled “Paid reader submission” and display the supplied author, area and category. Comments are enabled under published stories, subject to the usual moderation rules. Every submission is checked by a human editor for factual accuracy, relevance, formatting, spelling, grammar, image rights and compliance before publication. Payment does not guarantee publication; unsuitable submissions may be declined.

What we can and cannot publish

Submit community updates, achievements, local events, positive business milestones and neighbourhood stories. We do not accept stories covering murder, sexuality, sexual assault, historic or future crime, or any other crime-related allegations. Swearing is not permitted. Do not identify private individuals without their permission or make unverified accusations. Submitters must own or have permission to use all supplied material.

Images: Upload your own licensed photograph or choose an appropriate image from Rochdale Daily's existing image gallery. Editors make the final selection. The gallery option does not guarantee a specific image.
Minimum 250 characters, maximum 9,000 characters. We may edit for house style, grammar and clarity without changing the meaning.
PNG, JPEG or WebP, maximum 250 KB. You must own the image or have permission to use it.

Payment is collected by SumUp after submission. Keep the payment receipt and reference for verification. Nothing publishes automatically, including after payment.
