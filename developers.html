Public API

Read-only feeds for local news, live coverage, community events and the weather in the Rochdale borough. No key is needed.

Endpoints

AddressWhat it returns
GET /articles.jsonEvery published story, newest first: headline, summary, area, ward, photo, credit, source and the story body as HTML.
GET /api/live-updates?index=1Stories with live coverage running now.
GET /api/live-updates?slug=<slug>The key points and timestamped updates for one live story.
GET /api/eventsCommunity events approved by the newsdesk that have not yet finished, soonest first.
GET /weather.jsonThe Met Office forecast for Rochdale, by day.
GET /api/healthok, degraded (no new story for six hours) or down.

Machine-readable descriptions

The full OpenAPI 3.1 description is at /openapi.json. An API catalog (RFC 9727) is at /.well-known/api-catalog.

curl https://rochdaledaily.co.uk/api/live-updates?index=1

Using our journalism

Story text and photos stay the copyright of Rochdale Daily or the credited source. You may show headlines and short summaries with a link back to the story. Please don't republish full stories or photos without asking. Poll at most once a minute; the feeds are cached.
