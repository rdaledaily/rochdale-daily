Death notices, funerals and registering a death in Rochdale

Where to find death and funeral notices for the Rochdale borough, how to register a death, and the practical details families need: crematoriums, cemeteries, funeral directors and help with costs.

Checked 18 November 2026 · All guides

Where to find death notices now

Rochdale Online stopped trading in March 2025 and its notices page is no longer updated. The old address now carries gambling promotion, so please do not rely on it for funeral details.

  • Rochdale Daily publishes death notices and obituaries free, forever at rochdaledaily.co.uk/notices. Families and funeral directors can send a notice to news@rochdaledaily.co.uk.
  • Funeral notices placed in the Rochdale Observer appear online at funeral-notices.co.uk. Search by surname or by Rochdale.
  • Funeral directors usually hold the date, time and place of a service, and where donations should go. Their details are listed below.
  • Notices to creditors about a person's estate are published in The Gazette.

Notices on Rochdale Daily cost nothing and stay online permanently. We check every notice with the funeral director or family before it goes up.

Registering a death

A death must be registered in the district where it happened. For deaths in the Rochdale borough, that is Rochdale Register Office.

  1. The medical examiner or the coroner emails the paperwork to the register office. You will be told when this has happened.
  2. Phone 01706 924784 to book an appointment. You must register within five days of that email.
  3. At the appointment you can buy death certificates. The register office then emails the ‘green form’ to your funeral director.
AddressRochdale Register Office, Number One Riverside, Smith Street, Rochdale OL16 1XU
Phone01706 924784
Emailregister.office@rochdale.gov.uk
HoursMonday to Friday, 9am to 4.15pm (10.30am to 4.15pm on the last Thursday of the month). Appointment only.
CostRegistering is free. A death certificate costs £12.50.

The registrar will also offer Tell Us Once, which lets you tell the DWP, HMRC, DVLA, the Passport Office and council services such as Council Tax and Blue Badges in one go. It does not tell social housing landlords: contact the landlord yourself.

Bring the person's National Insurance number and date of birth, and if you have them, their driving licence, passport and Blue Badge.

Crematoriums and cemeteries

The council runs two crematoriums, at Rochdale (Rochdale Cemetery, Bury Road, OL11 4DG) and Middleton. The council's bereavement service lists cemeteries including Rochdale Cemetery, Heywood Cemetery, Middleton Cemetery on Boarshaw Road and Dearnley Cemetery in Littleborough.

Cremation services are held Monday to Friday between 9.30am and 4pm, usually on the hour. Each slot allows about 20 minutes for the service itself. There are no weekend services.

Help with funeral costs

If you are on a low income and responsible for a funeral, you may qualify for a Funeral Expenses Payment from the Department for Work and Pensions. How much you get depends on your circumstances.
