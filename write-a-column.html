Residents' Corner · Guest columns

Write a guest column

Up to 700 words, in your own name, on something that matters to people in the borough. The editor reads every column before it is published.

Before you write

  • Between 150 and 700 words, written by you. Facts and figures should come with a source we can check.
  • Your column is published under your name with a one-line description, and labelled as your own view.
  • Declare any interest: your party, employer, or any organisation affected by what you write about.
  • No personal attacks, and no claims about named people that you cannot prove. Anyone you criticise by name is offered a reply.
  • Columns about a criminal case that is still going through the courts cannot be published.
  • We may cut for length, house style or legal reasons, and will check with you before any change of substance.

Your column

Your email address is only used to confirm it is really you. It is never published.

Photo (optional) One picture: a photo of you, or one that goes with your column. It is resized on your device before sending, which also removes location data.

Privacy: we keep your email address only to confirm your submission and contact you about it. It is never shown on the site. See our privacy notice. Prefer email? Send your column to news@rochdaledaily.co.uk.
