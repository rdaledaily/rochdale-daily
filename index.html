    A volunteer-run branch covering the OL11, OL12, OL15, OL16 and M24 postcodes. It rehomes local cats and kittens, helps with lost and found, and funds neutering for households on benefits or facing financial hardship.

    • Cats Protection Rochdale · 03000 12 06 12Answered every day except Wednesday, when the branch is closed.
