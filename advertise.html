Before you decide

The practical questions.

Do you reach Whitworth as well as Rochdale?

Our core editorial focus is Rochdale borough, with neighbouring Whitworth stories when locally relevant. We cannot yet verify a specific number of Whitworth readers or promise geographic ad targeting. We will explain where a proposed advert actually appears.

Are adverts shown to every visitor?

No. Homepage banners rotate with other participating advertisers. Bespoke placements have their own agreed inventory. Campaign reporting is an estimate of displays and clicks, not unique people reached.

Can I try one month first?

Yes. The homepage banner is sold for one future calendar month. Other packages can be scoped for an agreed month, subject to location, creative and availability. No automatic renewal occurs through this enquiry form.

Could sponsorship affect what the paper writes?

No. Paid placements are labelled and kept separate from editorial decisions. You cannot buy favourable news coverage, editorial endorsements or control of reporting.

What if I don't have artwork?

Start with your company name, website and what you want customers to know. We can discuss suitable creative formats, but won't commit to producing artwork until the service and deliverables are agreed.

Can you guarantee sales or leads?

No responsible publication can. We can agree the visibility you are purchasing, provide available campaign data, and help you assess whether to renew.
