How advertising works

Request, confirm, pay, publish. Send an enquiry or upload a proposed banner. We confirm the specific placement, dates, total price and payment instructions before taking payment. A request alone does not reserve a place; publication requires confirmed payment and approval.

What you are purchasing

Banner bookings run for one agreed future calendar month, with non-exclusive equal-probability rotation in the two named homepage positions. Directory and sponsorship packages have separately agreed deliverables. We do not guarantee a minimum number of impressions, sales, leads or clicks. Site and ad performance metrics are estimates.

Artwork and independence

Advertisers are responsible for the accuracy of their claims and for permission to use any supplied logos and images. We may request changes or refuse misleading, unlawful or unsuitable material, and will discuss a refund if a paid booking cannot be delivered as agreed. Paid placements are clearly labelled and do not influence editorial coverage.

Payments and renewals

No automatic subscription or renewal is created by these enquiries. Any extension must be agreed and paid for separately.
