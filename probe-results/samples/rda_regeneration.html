A Vision For Positive Change

In Rochdale we’re really proud of our history, which is why we’re doing everything we can to preserve it. By doing so, we’re making our town more liveable for today. We celebrate our past while looking to the future, creating bustling, connected public spaces and bringing new energy to our town.
